Automatic / manual review policy

For each user's block of 100 simple link/video submissions, 65 positions are randomly selected for automatic approval after a random 15–30 minute delay. The other 35 remain pending for admin review. Partial blocks can have a different percentage. No submission is automatically rejected. Proof and form tasks stay manual.

Processing runs while the backend is awake. After downtime, overdue jobs resume. This selection does not verify video watch duration.
